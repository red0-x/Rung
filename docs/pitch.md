# Rung: 4:45 pitch + live demo

> **Superseded for the video pitch** by the 3-speaker run sheet: https://claude.ai/artifact/Sc8C2fCNUFo2AyZkgF73cT (Dana hook, $49/rep/month, Host / Caller / Rep roles). Hold video: `docs/pitch-assets/on-hold.mp4`. This file keeps the long notes.

**One-liner:** Rung is Gmail Priority Inbox for phone support.
**Plain version:** An AI answers every support call instantly, remembers the caller, writes the rep a brief, and ranks everyone for a callback, most urgent first.
**Tagline:** Never on hold. Never a stranger.

**Setup on stage:** laptop screen shared (dashboard), webcam on you, phone on speaker held up to the webcam. If a teammate can drive the laptop while you talk and call, do that.

**The four points (say them in the opener, prove each in the demo, repeat them at the close):**
1. **Nobody holds.** The AI picks up instantly, and a human calls you back.
2. **The right call first.** Ranked by urgency, then dollar value, then minutes waiting.
3. **The rep never starts cold.** Every call arrives as a three-line brief.
4. **Never a stranger.** Call back and it already knows you.

Times are cumulative targets. **Bold** = say it. *Italic* = do it. [Brackets] = fill in before you go on.

## Why it's built this way

Based on [YC's Demo Day guide](https://www.ycombinator.com/blog/guide-to-demo-day-pitches/), [Paul Graham's "How to Present to Investors"](https://paulgraham.com/investors.html) and [speedrun's founder notes](https://speedrun.substack.com/p/how-to-pitch-in-under-2-min):

- **Plain one-liner in the first 30 seconds.** YC: say what you do in words anyone gets, and don't bury the lead. The comparison makes it memorable, and the plain sentence right after makes it clear.
- **Three or four points, repeated.** YC says audiences keep about three or four points, and speedrun founders say one or two. So we pick four, say them in the opener, prove each in the demo, and repeat them at the close.
- **A personal hook.** speedrun's test: if someone else could give your pitch, it isn't personal enough. Use a true story.
- **Demo early, as proof.** Hackathon judges weigh the demo most. It starts at 0:40.
- **Why now, business model, team.** YC wants a business model by the end. speedrun asks for "why now" and tells founders to brag about the team. One line each.
- **End with a bang.** YC: tell the audience exactly what to remember.

## Don't say on stage

- **"Verified with an SMS code."** Code verification is off (D6, carrier registration not cleared). Say *"knew my number"*, never "verified".
- **"It texts you your callback time."** It's built, but carriers block texts to normal US phones for now. Q&A only.
- Don't point at Keewano numbers for the live call. They lag about 5 minutes.
- Don't quote a number for rep time saved. The app measures caller time only.

## Likely judge questions

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| How is this different from an IVR or a voicebot? | IVRs route you into a hold line. Rung ends the hold line: it answers, briefs, ranks, and remembers. |
| What if caller ID is spoofed? | The number only recognizes you. Sensitive requests (money movement, account changes) aren't discussed until a human calls back, and the code check is built in for when carriers clear us. |
| Privacy? | Memory is per company, analytics carry no names or numbers, and admins can delete a caller. |
| Why not Zendesk or Intercom? | They manage tickets after a human answers. Rung handles the call before any human does, and hands them a ranked, briefed list. Rung could plug into them. |
| What next? | Carrier registration for texts, CRM integrations, and routing by rep skill. |

## T−15 minutes checklist

1. **Make call 1 a true first call:** Callers → your row → ⋯ → **Forget caller…**. Don't use `/api/dev/reset`, which wipes the whole org.
2. Check the three seed rows are in the queue (fraud $4,200, $200K opening, $12 dispute).
3. A teammate makes 2–3 calls to the bank **from their own phones** with mixed urgency, and presses Call back on one. Then press ↻ on Insights.
4. Confirm the prompts are republished on both numbers after the last deploy.
5. Phone: full volume, Do Not Disturb on, speaker tested against the room mic.
6. Browser: logged out, at www.tryrung.today, zoomed to 125%.
7. Fill in every [bracket]: N and M from the Time returned tile, the personal hook, pricing, team lines.
8. Rehearse with a timer at least twice. speedrun founders scripted every word.

## If something breaks

| Problem | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| Agent slow or call fails | "Live phones at a hackathon, a classic." Play the backup recording of both calls (record it before 9 PM). |
| Row doesn't appear mid-call | Keep talking. Point at it after hangup. |
| Call 2 doesn't greet by name | You likely said no to "remember me." Open Callers and show the history. |
| Insights says "Keewano isn't connected" | Skip it and stay on the Time returned tile. |

## If you're running long

- Over at 2:50: drop Take next and keep the aging line.
- Over at 3:20: drop the Insights tab and keep the Time returned tile.
- Never cut: the second call's greeting, or the close.
